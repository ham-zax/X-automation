# Selected-behavior experiment, 29 October

Two DeepSeek V4.1 Flash Free max calls compare the same AndroidHarness source.
The frozen packets differ only in selected behavior. Persona, source, Writer
contract, schema and generation settings remain the same. No style fragment is
added. The social treatment changes purpose, mode, depth and reason together.
Both outputs and actual execution receipts are saved in their directories.

Judgment output:

> the phone part is the demo. the tool cards are the product — every call gets a diff sheet and its recorded check outcome instead of a hidden edit. strip that out and it's just another chat window in your pocket.

Social output:

> fixing a bug from the phone, then confirming the commit landed on the remote — that's the pitch and it's a good one. the resume cards are the unglamorous bit that decides whether it holds up.

The social opening responds more directly to the idea. Its second sentence still
announces the feature that decides whether the product holds up. The selected
social behavior does not prevent the model from inventing a decisive test.
The judgment output also combines tool-call cards and available diff sheets into
a stronger per-call capability claim. The source does not establish that claim.

The parent assesses these spans directly. No new model judge, numeric voice
score, production gate or public action is involved. Both returned field sets
match the requested fields. This check does not replace full schema validation.
Recorded activity contains no tool actions. Instructions are not an OS sandbox.

Conclusion: changing selected behavior alone does not remove the grading habit
in this pair. One sample per treatment cannot establish rates or exclude a
behavior effect. Do not declare persona or model capability the root cause.
Budget exhausted at two of two dispatches. No production change is accepted.

The next proposed discriminating check removes only the persona's systems and
recovery framing, with the social packet otherwise fixed. An earlier smaller
pilot also tested related cues without eliminating the habit. Review that
negative evidence before authorizing another similar call. Further prompt
changes need a specific new hypothesis rather than another general style rule.
