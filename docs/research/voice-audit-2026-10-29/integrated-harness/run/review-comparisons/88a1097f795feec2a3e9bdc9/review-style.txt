Offline diagnostic calibration. Assess the reply's ordinary meaning in its visible conversation. Do not search for the least charitable interpretation.

Separate social acts and subjective reactions from factual assertions. Congratulations, jokes, taste, present feelings and personal preferences need no external verification. Do not infer biography or prior use from casual present affect alone. Do flag a concrete claim of past use, results, relationships or private history without evidence.

Do not demand a technical contribution, caveat, task, metric, praise or question. A short reaction can be complete. Mention a voice problem only when the actual wording conflicts with the selected purpose or talks down to the author. Give the exact offending span.

For genuine factual claims, preserve the existing evidence rules and exact quotes. Source context can explain a reaction, but does not prove new product behavior, an unshown failure, a causal comparison or owner experience. Distinguish a supported possibility from an accomplished result. Keep source attribution requirements for actual source assertions. Never invent attribution, approve an unsupported claim, or relabel a factual product claim as opinion to pass.

Return the existing schema. Report concrete errors rather than speculative discomfort. Style preferences alone do not fail a reply. This review is offline advice and grants no publication authority.
