only Luna5.6. One availability worker precedes six writing calls. Actual receipts
report gpt-6-luna and reasoningEffort=max. No model substitution occurs.

Both request files match the earlier full/lean experiment byte for byte. Three
samples per arm use source VA-16 and the fixed social behavior. The user authorizes
parallel work. Calls run in pairs, with the existing GLM work alongside them.
Logical order and labels remain full, lean, lean, full, full, lean. Wall-clock
execution differs from earlier serial comparisons and remains a limitation.

## Parent assessment

Full replies are present preferences or interested reactions. None announces an
invented product acceptance test. None claims prior owner usage. Some are generic
or follow the source's opening idea closely. Natural tone does not establish
originality, owner acceptance or contribution value in every conversation.

Lean replies are also conversational, with more feature acknowledgment. Reply2
preserves the source's diff sheets and recorded check outcomes reasonably. Replies3
and6 use “diff cards” or “diff and check cards,” while the source distinguishes
tool-call cards from available diff sheets and recorded outcomes. This is loose
UI terminology, not a demonstrated per-call quantifier error. It deserves a
factual wording note rather than a claim that every output is perfectly grounded.

The narrow earlier label, explicit imposed test or unsupported competitive test,
appears in zero of three full and zero of three lean replies in parent assessment.
That is not an objective quality score or estimated failure rate. One source and
three samples per arm do not establish a model ranking, stable persona fit or
24x7 readiness. Provider and effort also differ from the DeepSeek reference.

All six outputs pass the real structured-output validator. Activity records show
no tool actions. Exact receipts and outputs are saved. Usage and money remain
unknown. The availability worker is not a generation sample. No production
writing contract, persona, policy, browser, state or public send changes.

Luna6, like Luna5.6, can produce peer reactions under the full packet in this set.
The larger packet therefore is not a sufficient explanation for all grading.
The result does not establish a DeepSeek capability ceiling or justify adopting
another model without the owner's decision. The different contract proposed by
the other agent remains untested.
